5. 语法知识点（本次最高频）

时态一致性——回忆在美国的经历一律用过去时
规则：写信时回忆当年在美国的事，全部用过去时，不能混现在时/将来时。
错误示例："It's you that greeted me" / "you will appear like an angel"
正确示例："it was you who greeted me" / "you would always show up"

6. 完善后文章（保守提分版，建议考试用）

Dear Ally,

It has been one month since I returned from the U.S. Whenever I think back to my exchange experience in the U.S., your face comes to mind. I am writing to express my sincere gratitude for your warm hospitality.

When I first arrived in the U.S., it was you who greeted me with a smile and put me at ease. You quickly became my closest friend in the Sino-American cultural exchange program. As a newcomer, I often felt confused, and you would always show up like an angel. Since I returned, I have missed you every single day.

I would like to warmly welcome you to visit China when the time is right. I will take you to visit many historic sites, which will give you a deep impression of Chinese history.

In closing, I would like to express my heartfelt thanks again for your help and hospitality. I sincerely hope you can visit China one day.

Yours,
Zhang Wei
